# A new season

Before opening the orchard, inspect the gate and check that all volunteers have a safe route to the packing shed.

# Orchard field guide

In the north orchard, early pears ripen after cool nights. Keep a dated notebook for each tree and record rainfall, soil moisture, and the colour of the fruit before harvest.

A healthy root system needs air as well as water. Clear dense weeds around young trees, leave a small ring of exposed soil beside the trunk, and check the drainage after heavy rain.

Pruning opens the canopy to sunlight. Remove crossing branches with clean tools during the dormant season. Leave useful fruiting spurs intact and carry the cuttings away from the orchard.

A shared harvest calendar helps volunteers plan their work. Record the varieties in each row, agree on the next inspection date, and put clean baskets beside the packing shed.

Store ripe fruit in shallow trays. Check each tray regularly and remove damaged fruit before it affects the rest. Label the harvest date and keep the storage room cool and ventilated.

Review the season in a short meeting. Compare the notes for different rows, note which tasks took longer than expected, and choose one useful improvement for next year's harvest.
